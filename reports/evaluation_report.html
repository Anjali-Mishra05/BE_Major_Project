How to read this

At 25.00% fraud, accuracy is not a useful metric. The majority-class dummy — which labels every transaction legitimate and needs no training — reaches 75.00% accuracy while catching zero fraud. The metrics that matter are ROC-AUC, PR-AUC and recall, compared against the dummy and random scorer above. The label-correlated behaviour column is excluded from training; see reports/baseline_results.md.
