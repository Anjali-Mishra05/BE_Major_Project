How to read this

At 0.19% fraud, accuracy is not a useful metric. The majority-class dummy — which labels every transaction legitimate and needs no training — reaches 99.81% accuracy while catching zero fraud. The metrics that matter are ROC-AUC, PR-AUC and recall, and the trained model lands at the random-scorer values on all three. This is the documented Phase I finding: the dataset's fraud_flag carries no learnable relationship to its features.
